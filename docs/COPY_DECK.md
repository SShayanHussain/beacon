# Copy Deck — Beacon Light Publishing

## Brand voice

Beacon Light talks like a good editor: calm, specific, never breathless. The category shouts ("Your bestseller awaits!"). We don't, because the audience is already anxious and a raised voice reads as a sales floor.

**Do:** name the thing plainly. Use numbers only when they're real. Say what happens next. Short sentences carry weight; save the long one for when it matters.

**Don't:** "unleash", "embark on a journey", "in today's fast-paced world", "we're passionate about", exclamation marks in headings, or any claim that would need an asterisk.

**One-line positioning:** *You wrote it. We help you get it into the world, and you keep every right and every royalty.*

---

## Integrity rules

Everything on this site must be true and, if challenged, provable.

- Any unverified stat, award, client count, or testimonial is written as `PLACEHOLDER: <what's needed>` in the data file and set `verified: false`. Those render only in development, behind a dashed amber outline.
- Sections built on data (stat rows, review walls, logo strips) return `null` when there's no verified data. An absent section always beats an invented one.
- Never name a retailer or partner we don't actually distribute through.
- Never imply a traditional-publisher relationship. The rights notice appears in the footer of every page.

**Eyebrow** `↳ WHY THIS EXISTS`

**Body** The hard part of publishing was never the upload button — that's free and takes ten minutes. The hard part is everything that decides whether a stranger finishes page one: an edit that respects your voice, a cover that reads at thumbnail size, metadata that puts the book in front of people who already want it. We do those things, we quote them up front, and we hand the finished book back to you entirely.

**Principles**
1. **You keep 100%.** Rights, royalties, intellectual property. We're work-for-hire — we never take a share of what your book earns.
2. **Fixed scope, fixed price.** You see the deliverables and the number before anything starts. No mid-project surprises.
3. **A named human.** One editor, one designer, one project lead. You'll know who is reading your manuscript.

- **Book Editing** — Developmental, line, and copy editing that sharpens your voice instead of flattening it.

### Process
**Eyebrow** `↳ THE ROUTE`
**H2** Six waypoints from manuscript to shelf.

1. **Manuscript review** — Send what you have, in whatever format. We read it and come back with a scope, a timeline, and a price.
2. **Editing** — Developmental notes first, then line editing, then a copyedit. You approve each pass.
3. **Design** — Cover concepts built for thumbnail legibility, then interior typesetting for print and ebook.
4. **Registration** — ISBN, barcode, copyright filing, and metadata. Your name on every record.
5. **Distribution** — Print and digital files delivered to your retail accounts. The accounts stay in your name.
6. **Launch** — A dated plan for the first thirty days, and the assets to run it.
