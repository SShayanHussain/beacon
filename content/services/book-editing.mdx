Editing is not about changing your book into something else; it is about making it the best possible version of what you intended.

<Quote author="Hiba" role="Lead Editor">
  A good editor is invisible. The reader should never know we were there.
</Quote>

### The three passes

Many freelancers bundle line editing and copyediting together, which often leads to errors slipping through. We treat them as distinct processes.

1. **Developmental:** We look at the skeleton. Does the narrative hold up?
2. **Line:** We look at the muscle. Does the prose move well?
3. **Copy:** We look at the skin. Are there any blemishes?

You remain in control throughout. We use Track Changes in Microsoft Word, allowing you to see every suggestion and ultimately decide what makes it into the final book.
