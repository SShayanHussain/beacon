When an author finishes a manuscript, they often face a choice: give up their rights and a chunk of their royalties to a traditional publisher, or navigate a sea of freelancers and platforms on their own.

<Callout type="note">
  Beacon Light was built to offer a third way: the quality of a traditional publishing house, with the ownership and control of self-publishing.
</Callout>

### Quality without the compromise

A book needs to look, feel, and read like a traditionally published book to compete in the market. Readers do not grade on a curve for self-published authors.

We manage the entire process — from the first developmental edit to the final file upload on retail platforms. You get a dedicated team of editors, designers, and formatters working on your book, all managed by a single point of contact.

### The accounts are yours

Unlike hybrid publishers or vanity presses, we do not act as the publisher of record. We set up your accounts on Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, and other platforms using your email and your bank details. 

<Checklist items={[
  "100% of royalties go directly to you",
  "You hold the copyright and all subsidiary rights",
  "Your name is on every retail account",
  "You retain control over pricing and promotions"
]} />

If you ever decide to take your book elsewhere, you do not need our permission. The accounts, the files, and the rights are yours.
