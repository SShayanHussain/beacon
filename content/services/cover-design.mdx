A book cover is not an art piece; it is a packaging decision. It is the single most important marketing asset you have.

### The one-second test

When a reader is scrolling through Amazon, they give a book cover about one second of attention. In that second, the cover must communicate the genre, the tone, and the professional quality of the book. 

If the typography looks amateur, or the imagery confuses the genre, the reader scrolls past, no matter how good the writing inside is.

<Steps>
  <Step number={1} title="The Front Cover">
    Designed primarily for digital display. The title must be legible when the image is small.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="The Full Wrap">
    For the paperback and hardcover. We design the spine and back cover, integrating the barcode and blurb.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="The Interior">

</Steps>
