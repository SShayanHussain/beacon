Launch week is exciting, but it is just one week. The reality of publishing is that most successful books make their money in the months and years *after* they are released.

<Callout type="warning">
  A great book with terrible metadata will not sell, simply because no one can find it.
</Callout>

### Playing the algorithm

We focus heavily on Amazon, because that is where the readers are. This means treating your book's metadata — its title, subtitle, series name, description, categories, and backend keywords — with as much care as the manuscript itself.

We write descriptions that hook readers and include the keywords Amazon needs to index your book properly.

### The slow work

After launch, we manage the slow work of staying discoverable. This involves setting up and tuning Amazon Ads (managing ACOS to ensure profitability) and coordinating price promotions (like BookBub or Freebooksy) to spike the algorithm.
