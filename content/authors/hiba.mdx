I have spent the last decade in independent publishing, working on both sides of the manuscript: as a developmental editor shaping the narrative, and as a project manager getting the final files onto retail shelves.

Beacon Light is the company I wish existed when I first started in this industry. It is built on a single, uncompromising principle: the author keeps the rights, and the author keeps the royalties. We are paid for our work, once.

When I am not working on books, I am usually reading them.
