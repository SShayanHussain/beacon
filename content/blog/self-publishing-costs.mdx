It costs zero dollars to upload a Word document and a JPEG to Amazon KDP and hit 'Publish'. That is the most dangerous fact in modern publishing.

Because the *upload* is free, authors often assume the *process* should be free. But creating a book that readers actually want to buy requires investment.

### Where the money goes

If you want a book that looks and reads like it belongs on a bookstore shelf, you are going to spend money in three main areas:

<Steps>
  <Step number={1} title="Editing">
    This is usually the largest expense. Developmental editing, line editing, and copyediting are distinct skills that take a lot of time.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Design">
    Cover design and interior formatting. A good cover designer is worth their weight in gold. 
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Marketing">
    Advertising (Amazon Ads, Facebook Ads), ARC services, and promotional newsletters (like BookBub or BargainBooksy).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Where to save and where to splurge

If your budget is tight, **do not skimp on the cover or the copyedit.** 

Readers will forgive a slightly plain interior layout. They will not forgive a typo in the first paragraph, and they will never even see the first paragraph if the cover looks amateurish.
