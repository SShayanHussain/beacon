# Privacy Policy

We collect the minimum amount of information necessary to provide our services and communicate with you. We do not sell your data.

## Information we collect

When you use our contact form or book a call, we collect your name, email address, and any project details you choose to share. This information is used solely to evaluate your manuscript and provide a quote.

## Third-party services

We use a few trusted third-party services to run our business:

- **Vercel** for hosting the website.
- **Resend** for delivering form submissions via email.
- **Cal.com** for scheduling calls.

These services have their own privacy policies. We do not use tracking pixels, Google Analytics, or third-party advertising cookies on this site.
